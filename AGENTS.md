# Website image updates

After replacing an image or changing its displayed size, inspect the actual page in a browser at desktop and mobile widths before reporting completion. Check that the image loads, retains its aspect ratio, has no unintended cropping, and has a balanced size relative to its card and surrounding text. Reviewing the image file alone is insufficient.

Keep the ongoing research cards compact and side by side on desktop, with equally sized image areas. Verify the layout again after changing to an image with a different aspect ratio.
